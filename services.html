What we offer

Programs designed for the realities of gambling operations.

Every program is developed with the gambling industry in mind — the culture, the pressures, the workforce, and the regulatory environment your organization operates in.

Program 01

Awareness & Recognition Training

Most people in crisis don't announce it. The signs are there — but only if you know what to look for, and only if you feel safe enough to act on what you see.

This foundational workshop equips frontline managers and team leads with the awareness and language to recognize when a colleague may be struggling with substance use — and the confidence to respond with care rather than alarm.

Half-day workshop • In-person or virtual • Up to 20 participants

What's covered

  • Understanding addiction as a health issue, not a moral failure
  • Recognizing behavioural and performance signs of substance use
  • The difference between concern and accusation
  • How gambling-specific stressors contribute to vulnerability
  • Practical language for starting a conversation without judgment
  • When to escalate, and to whom
  • Documentation and duty of care basics

Who this is for

Frontline supervisors, floor managers, pit bosses, shift leaders, and anyone in a direct oversight role across gambling operations, hospitality, and security.

Program 02

Manager Response & Disclosure Training

When someone finally reaches out for help, what happens in that first conversation matters enormously. A response that feels clumsy, judgmental, or procedural can close a door that took years to open.

This advanced workshop prepares managers and HR leaders to handle disclosure with empathy, clarity, and confidence — protecting both the employee and the organization.

Full-day workshop • In-person or virtual • Up to 16 participants

What's covered

  • The psychology of disclosure — what it costs to come forward
  • How to respond in the moment, including what not to say
  • Setting boundaries while maintaining compassion
  • Navigating performance and attendance concerns alongside support
  • Connecting employees to the right resources and referral pathways
  • Confidentiality, privacy obligations, and legal considerations
  • Supporting the team around someone in recovery
  • Scenario-based practice with facilitated debrief

Who this is for

HR business partners, department heads, senior managers, and people leaders responsible for employee wellbeing decisions.

Program 03

Stigma Reduction & Culture Programs

Training workshops address the individual. Culture programs address the organization. If the environment itself is hostile to vulnerability, individual skills can only go so far.

These programs work at the organizational level to reduce stigma, build psychological safety, and create the structural conditions that allow people to stay tethered to their workplace rather than drift away in silence.

Multi-session engagement • Tailored to your organization • Executive + team levels

What's included

  • Organizational readiness assessment
  • Executive and leadership alignment sessions
  • All-team awareness sessions (facilitated, non-clinical)
  • Policy review and recommendations
  • Internal referral pathway mapping
  • Language and communication guidelines for your organization
  • Ongoing advisory support (quarterly check-ins)

Who this is for

Organizations committed to a sustained, organization-wide approach — typically as a follow-on to initial training or in preparation for Tether Certification.

Delivery options

We work the way you work

In-Person

Facilitated on-site at your property or head office. Best for hands-on scenario practice and building team cohesion around this topic.

Virtual

Live, facilitator-led sessions delivered online. Ideal for distributed teams, multi-property groups, or scheduling flexibility.

Hybrid

A combination of in-person and virtual sessions — typically an in-person launch followed by virtual follow-up and reinforcement.
