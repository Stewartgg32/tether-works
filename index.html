What we do

Built for the people who keep the gambling industry running

The gambling floor is a high-pressure environment. Irregular hours, shift work, and financial stress create real vulnerabilities. Tether Works was built specifically for this industry — because generic workplace wellness programs don't account for the realities of your world.

We train frontline managers, HR leaders, and executives to identify when a colleague may be struggling with drug or alcohol addiction, understand exactly what to do when someone comes forward, and foster a culture where asking for help is an act of strength — not a career risk.

See our programs

Recognition Training

Learn to identify the early signs of addiction in a colleague — before a crisis — and understand the difference between performance concerns and underlying need.

Response & Support

Know what to say — and what not to say — when someone comes to you for help. Navigate disclosure, boundaries, and next steps with confidence and compassion.

Stigma & Culture

Build a team culture where connection comes first. Reduce the fear, shame, and silence that prevent people from reaching out when they need it most.

Our philosophy

The opposite of addiction is not sobriety. It is connection.

Research has shown that human connection — feeling seen, valued, and tethered to the people around us — is the most powerful protective factor against addiction. Our work is not about discipline or detection. It is about helping organizations become the kind of place where people want to stay.
