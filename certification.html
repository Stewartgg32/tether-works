Tether Certification

Demonstrate your commitment. Formalize your culture.

The Tether Certification recognizes gaming organizations that have built the knowledge, policies, and culture to support employees facing addiction — and are willing to stand behind that commitment publicly.

What certification means

More than a badge. A commitment.

The Tether Certification is not a self-assessment checklist. It is a structured program that verifies your organization has completed the training, put in place the right policies, and built the internal capability to support employees navigating addiction.

Certified organizations receive the Tether Works mark — a signal to employees, recruits, industry peers, and regulators that your workplace takes this seriously.

Certification is renewed annually, reflecting your organization's ongoing commitment rather than a one-time exercise.

The path to certification

Four steps to becoming Tether Certified

1

Complete foundational training

A minimum percentage of your management and HR team must complete Tether Works' Awareness & Recognition and Manager Response training programs. We'll establish the right threshold based on your organization's size.

2

Policy and pathway review

We review your existing HR policies, employee assistance program (EAP) arrangements, and referral pathways. We provide a written gap assessment and work with you to address any deficiencies.

3

Culture assessment

A brief, anonymous survey of your workforce gives us a baseline picture of psychological safety, stigma levels, and awareness. This forms part of the certification record and informs your renewal journey.

4

Certification awarded

Upon successful completion, your organization receives the Tether Certified mark, a formal certificate, and listing on the Tether Works certified organization directory. Certification is valid for 12 months.

Why it matters

Certification benefits your organization — and your people.

Talent & retention

People want to work for organizations that care. Certification signals that your company is serious about employee wellbeing — a powerful differentiator in a competitive labour market.

Regulatory confidence

Gaming regulators increasingly expect operators to demonstrate duty-of-care toward employees. Tether Certification provides documented evidence of structured, proactive support.

Real outcomes

Beyond the badge: organizations that invest in addiction-aware culture see measurable reductions in absenteeism, turnover, and productivity loss associated with untreated substance use.

Common questions

Frequently asked

How long does certification take?

Most organizations complete the certification process within 3–6 months, depending on organizational size, scheduling, and how much policy work is required. We'll give you a realistic timeline during the initial consultation.

What if we already have some training or policies in place?

Great — we start by assessing what you have. Existing training and policies count toward certification requirements where they meet the standard. We're not here to make you redo work that's already good.

Is certification available for multi-property organizations?

Yes. We offer both property-level certification and enterprise-wide certification for multi-site operators. Contact us to discuss the right approach for your organizational structure.

What does renewal involve?

Annual renewal includes a brief check-in on training currency, policy updates, and a short culture pulse survey. For most certified organizations, renewal is a relatively light-touch process — the hard work is in the initial certification.
