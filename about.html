Why we exist

The science of connection

For decades, addiction was treated as a moral failure — a personal weakness to be corrected through willpower and consequence. We now know that story was wrong.

Research consistently shows that social isolation is one of the most powerful drivers of addictive behaviour. The conditions that fuel addiction — loneliness, disconnection, shame — are also the conditions that prevent people from asking for help.

When workplaces are built on fear and silence, people who are struggling don't come forward. They drift. Quietly, and often for years, before a crisis forces the issue.

"The opposite of addiction is not sobriety. The opposite of addiction is connection."

— Johann Hari, Lost Connections

This is the principle Tether Works is built on. Our programs are not about surveillance or enforcement. They are about building the kind of connected, psychologically safe workplaces where people feel seen — and where reaching out for help is understood as an act of courage, not weakness.

The gambling industry presents unique challenges: shift work, irregular schedules, financial stress, and a culture that has historically valued toughness over vulnerability. We built Tether Works to address exactly that context.

Our roots

Connected to Recovery in Gaming

Tether Works operates alongside Recovery in Gaming, a broader movement dedicated to supporting people in the gambling industry who are navigating recovery from addiction.

While Recovery in Gaming connects individuals with peer support and community, Tether Works focuses on the organizational side — ensuring that gambling employers are equipped to be part of the solution.
